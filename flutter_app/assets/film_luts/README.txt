darkmoon film look tables
=========================

The film_*.png files are 33x33x33 colour look-up tables in darkmoon's
packed layout (see lib/render/film_lut.dart), resampled by
tool/build_film_luts.dart from the RawTherapee Film Simulation Collection
(version 2015-09-20) by Pat David, Pavlov Dmitry and Michael Ezra:

  https://rawtherapee.com/shared/HaldCLUT.zip
  https://rawpedia.rawtherapee.com/Film_Simulation

The film names in manifest.json are used for informational purposes only:
they identify which film stock each table is designed to approximate.
