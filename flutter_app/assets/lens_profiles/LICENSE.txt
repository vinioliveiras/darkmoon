Lens correction data bundled with Darkmoon (lensfun_db.json)
==============================================================

This asset is a compact, machine-generated conversion of the lens/camera
calibration database from the Lensfun project:

    https://github.com/lensfun/lensfun

Source: lensfun's data/db/*.xml files (distortion, vignetting, and
transverse chromatic aberration calibration points only -- mount
compatibility lists, camera-body entries, and calibration comments were
dropped as unused by this app).

it) must be shared under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 license.

This JSON encoding was produced by a one-off conversion script (not
shipped with the app) from an unmodified checkout of Lensfun's data/db
directory.
